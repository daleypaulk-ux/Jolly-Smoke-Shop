// Our Story

More Than a
Smoke Shop

Jolly Smoke Shop opened with a simple idea: Springfield deserved a smoke shop that felt like it was run by neighbors, not a corporation. We're a family-owned business, from the person who greets you at the counter to the person restocking the shelves.

We picked 1531 W Walnut St because it's central, easy to get to, and close to the people we wanted to serve. Since opening, we've stayed true to that mission — stocking a huge selection, pricing things fairly, and never rushing a customer out the door.

And because life doesn't run on a 9-to-5 schedule, neither do we. We're open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so whenever you need us, we're already open.
